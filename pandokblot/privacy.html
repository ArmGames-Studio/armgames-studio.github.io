Pandok Blot Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

In short: to let you play online with friends, Pandok Blot keeps a small player account on our server: your player name, the picture you choose, your game progress and your friends. We do not ask for your email address, phone number or contacts, and we never sell your data. The game may show ads from Google AdMob. You can delete your account at any time in the game's Settings.

Who we are

Pandok Blot is a card game made by ArmGames, operated by Ashot Abrahamyan ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at ashot.abrahamyan@outlook.com.

Your account

You can play against the computer without an account. To play online, you get a player account in one of these ways:

  • Guest: an account created by the game, with no personal information. It lives on this device only: if you delete the app or lose the device, a guest account cannot be recovered.
  • Sign in with Apple: Apple gives us an anonymous user ID for our app. We do not receive or store your Apple email address or your real name.
  • Facebook Login: Facebook gives us your Facebook user ID for our app. We store only that ID. We do not read your Facebook friends and we never post to Facebook for you.

Signing in with Apple or Facebook keeps your progress when you change or reinstall your phone.

Data we store on our server

  • Profile: the player name you choose and a four-digit number added to it (for example "Aram #4821"), the character portrait you pick, and a profile photo if you upload one.
  • Birth year, if you give it. It is private and never shown to anyone. We use it to keep the game and its ads suitable for your age.
  • Game data: your level and experience points, your apricots (the game's coins) and their history, rewards you have collected, quests, the store items you own and use, your places on the leaderboards, your online match statistics and last online result, and how many online matches you have played with other players.
  • Friends: your friends list, friend requests and table invitations, and when you last used the game (your friends see whether you are online).
  • Reports: if you report a player's photo, or another player reports yours, we keep the report so we can review it.

Games against the computer, your settings, saved matches and replays are kept on your device only.

What other players can see

Your player name and number, your portrait or photo, your level, your leaderboard places and your profile statistics are visible to other players. Your friends also see when you are online or playing. Your birth year and your Apple or Facebook ID are never shown.

Technical data

Like most online services, our server and its web front end keep standard logs that include your IP address, the time and the request made. We use them only for security and to fix problems, and normally keep them no longer than 30 days. We do not use any analytics or crash-reporting service in the game.

How we use your data

We use the data above only to run the game: to keep your account and progress, let you play online, show friends, invitations and leaderboards, give rewards, keep the game fair and safe, and answer you when you contact us. We do not sell your data and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Pandok Blot is free and may show ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: Google Ireland Limited): sometimes a full-screen ad between matches, and optional "rewarded" videos that you choose to watch for free apricots.

To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information from your device, such as:

  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location (country or city);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • advertising interactions, such as which ads were shown and whether they were tapped;
  • the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (see below);
  • diagnostic data about the ad SDK's performance.

Google processes this information under its own privacy policy. You can learn more at policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not send your player account data to Google.

We limit ads in Pandok Blot to content suitable for a general audience (rated PG) and block categories such as dating, gambling and sexual content.

Your choices

Tracking permission (iPhone and iPad)

iOS may ask whether Pandok Blot can track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. You can change this at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Users in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland

We ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can accept, refuse, or choose specific purposes. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy option in the game's Settings.

Users in US states with privacy laws

Where state law gives you the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising, you can use the privacy option in the game's Settings to opt out.

Removing ads

You can buy "Remove ads" to stop the ads shown between matches. Rewarded videos stay available only if you choose to watch them.

Your profile

You can change your player name, portrait and photo, or remove your photo, at any time in your profile.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple tells the game only whether a purchase was successful, so it can give you the item.

Deleting your account

Open Settings in the game and choose "Delete account". Your account, your photo and your game data are deleted from our server straight away, and this cannot be undone. Deleting the app alone does not delete an account that is signed in with Apple or Facebook; you can also ask us by email to delete it.

Children

Pandok Blot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The game has no gambling for money: apricots can never be cashed out or exchanged for money or anything outside the game. If you are a parent and believe your child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and security

We keep your account data for as long as you have an account and delete it when you delete the account. Server logs are kept as described above. Data saved on your device stays until you delete the app. We protect the data with encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted access to the server, and a separate database with its own login used only by the game. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your data safe. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies.

International transfers

Our game server is hosted by DigitalOcean in the United States, so your account data is stored there. Google may also process advertising data in countries other than yours, with the safeguards described in its privacy policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. You can change or delete most of your data yourself in the game. For anything else, contact us at the address below and we will answer within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's update notes.
