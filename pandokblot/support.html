Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Pandok Blot is Armenian Bazar Blot: four players, two against two, with 32 cards. Your first game against the computer shows a short guide, and the rules of each table are in the game. Start with "Solo" on the home screen to practise against bots.

How do I keep my progress when I change my phone?

Open Settings and sign in with Apple or Facebook. Your level, apricots, items and friends are then kept on our server and come back when you sign in on the new phone. A guest account is tied to one device and cannot be recovered if the app is deleted.

How do I play with friends?

Open Friends from the side menu. Add a friend with their code (their name and number, for example "Aram #4821"), or share your own code. Then invite them to your table, or create a private table and share its code.

What are apricots?

Apricots are the game's coins. You earn them by playing, from the daily spin, quests and optional videos, and spend them on decks, venues, frames and emotes in the Store. They are for fun only: they can never be cashed out or exchanged for money.

How do I change my name or photo?

Open your profile and tap "Edit profile". You can change your name and portrait, upload a photo or remove it.

Someone has an offensive photo or name. What can I do?

Open the player's card and tap "Report photo", or email us with the player's name and number. We review every report.

How do I remove ads?

Buy "Remove ads" in the Store. It removes the ads shown between matches. Rewarded videos stay available if you want their apricots.

I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads between matches.

Open the Store and tap "Restore purchases" while signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings in the game and tap "Delete account". Your account and its data are deleted from our server straight away. You can also email us and we will do it for you.

How do I change my privacy choices for ads?

Open Settings in the game and choose the privacy option to change your ad consent. To change tracking permission, open the iOS Settings app, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking. See our Privacy Policy.
