Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Use the on-screen buttons: left and right on the left side, Jump and Dash on the right. Jump against a wall to wall-jump. You can also play with a game controller. You can change button size, position and transparency in Settings.

The game is too hard. Can I make it easier?

Yes. Open Settings and turn on Assist Mode. You can slow the game down, get an extra air dash, or become invincible.

Where is my progress saved?

Your progress and settings are saved on your device only. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted too.

How do I remove ads?

Buy "Remove Ads" in Settings. It removes the ads shown between levels. Optional rewarded ads stay available if you want their rewards.

I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads between levels.

Open Settings and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I change my privacy choices for ads?

Open Settings in the game and choose the privacy option to change your ad consent. To change tracking permission, open the iOS Settings app, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking. See our Privacy Policy.
