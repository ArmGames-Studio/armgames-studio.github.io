Lumen Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

In short: Lumen has no accounts and we do not collect your personal data. Your progress stays on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads, depending on the choices you make.

Who we are

Lumen is a game made by ArmGames, operated by Ashot Abrahamyan ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at ashot.abrahamyan@outlook.com.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal data. Lumen has no sign-in and no account. We do not run our own servers for the game and we do not receive your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Your game progress, best times, unlocked lantern colors and settings are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Lumen is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: Google Ireland Limited). We show a full-screen ad between some levels, and optional "rewarded" ads that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward.

To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information from your device, such as:

  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location (country or city);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • advertising interactions, such as which ads were shown and whether they were tapped;
  • the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (see below);
  • diagnostic data about the ad SDK's performance.

Google processes this information under its own privacy policy. You can learn more at policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

We limit ads in Lumen to content suitable for a general audience (rated PG) and block categories such as dating, gambling and sexual content.

Your choices

Tracking permission (iPhone and iPad)

The first time you play, iOS may ask whether Lumen can track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. You can change this at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Users in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland

We ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can accept, refuse, or choose specific purposes. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy option in the game's Settings.

Users in US states with privacy laws

Where state law gives you the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising, you can use the privacy option in the game's Settings to opt out.

Removing ads

You can buy "Remove Ads" to stop the ads shown between levels. Optional rewarded ads stay available only if you choose to watch them.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple tells the game only whether a purchase was successful, so it can unlock the item on your device.

Children

Lumen is a family-friendly game but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided personal data through the game, contact us and we will help.

Data retention and security

We do not hold personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Data saved on your device stays until you delete the app. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies.

International transfers

Google may process advertising data in countries other than yours, including the United States, with the safeguards described in its privacy policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google; we are also happy to help if you contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's update notes.
